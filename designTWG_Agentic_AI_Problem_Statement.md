# designTWG

## Problem Statement & Opportunity Definition
### Agentic AI Business Operating System

**Sales Growth | Design Operations | Project Profitability | CEO Intelligence**

October 2026 • Working concept paper for AI development partners, implementation planning and funding discussions

---

## 1. Executive problem statement

designTWG is a multidisciplinary branding and interior design firm delivering hospitality, franchise, restaurant, retail and commercial projects in Canada and the United States. The firm has established project processes, D1–D5 design workflows, checklists, templates and valuable historical knowledge. Its information is distributed across Zoho CRM, Zoho Projects, QuickBooks Online and Microsoft SharePoint.

The central challenge is the gap between available business information and consistent, timely, accountable execution. Critical work—including lead follow-up, proposal development, project monitoring, scope control, design quality checks and financial reporting—requires coordination across people and systems. This can contribute to revenue leakage, slow response times, preventable rework, margin erosion and excessive CEO intervention.

> The identified gaps below are hypotheses requiring validation through system audits, staff interviews and baseline measurement; they are not represented as proven failures.

---

## 2. Strategic objective

Develop a secure, human-supervised Agentic AI Business Operating System that can retrieve authorized information, interpret business context, detect exceptions, propose or initiate permitted workflows, seek approvals for consequential actions, and track outcomes. The goal is to improve conversion, delivery reliability, profitability and scalability without proportionate administrative overhead.

## 7. Recommended first proof of concept

Phase 1 should target **S01 (sales follow-up discipline)** and **O02 (proactive project monitoring)**, supported by **O09 (weekly CEO reporting)**. These workflows have identifiable source data in Zoho and measurable outcomes. The existing Monday 8:00 a.m. CEO review provides a practical management cadence.

1. Ingest approved CRM deal fields, project tasks and validated QuickBooks Online reporting snapshots.
2. Identify open opportunities without next actions and project milestones that are overdue or at risk.
3. Prepare exception lists, draft proposed follow-ups, and route them to Irwin, Nameeta or Anju for review.
4. Publish a traceable weekly CEO report with source dates, owners, decisions and next steps.
5. Log actions, approvals, errors and measured outcomes; avoid unsupervised external commitments.

## 8. Data and technical requirements

| System | Authoritative content | Integration approach |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zoho CRM | Leads, accounts, deals, stages, owners, next actions | Export-based pilot; later authorized API integration |
| Zoho Projects | Projects, tasks, deadlines, phase gates, assignees | Export-based pilot; later authorized API integration |
| QuickBooks Online | P&L, invoices, payments and AR aging | Approved reports first; read-only API later |
| SharePoint / Microsoft 365 | SOPs, drawings, templates, approved documents, reports | Controlled document access; Power Automate workflows |
| ChatGPT / Claude | Reasoning, drafting and document analysis | Approved workspace/API setup; no assumption of unattended subscription access |

**Technical safeguards:** least-privilege access, document-level permission enforcement, explicit tool permissions, source citations, human approval gates, audit logs, data retention rules, prompt-injection defenses, error handling and measurable evaluation tests.

## 10. Discovery questions and evidence needed

1. What percentage of open Zoho deals lack next steps or show no meaningful activity within the agreed threshold?
2. How long does proposal development take by project type, and what causes delays?
3. How many active project tasks are overdue, and which D1–D5 checks are inconsistently documented?
4. How often are additional scope requests completed without documented commercial approval?
5. Can project effort and financial records be reconciled to estimate realized margins?
6. Which documents and fields are reliable, current, and accessible under appropriate permissions?

## 11. Business case and intended outcomes

The expected benefits are stronger lead conversion, reduced administrative effort, more consistent project delivery, earlier identification of schedule and margin risks, and more CEO capacity for strategic development. Financial ROI must be calculated using validated baseline time, conversion, fee, cost and rework data; no savings or revenue uplift is guaranteed.

## 13. Formal development problem statement

designTWG seeks to develop a domain-specific, human-supervised agentic AI platform that bridges fragmented sales, project and financial systems with its established design processes. The platform will detect operational exceptions, synthesize context from authorized sources, recommend and execute approved workflows, and measure results. The research and implementation challenge is to achieve reliable, secure, auditable and commercially valuable multi-system coordination in a multidisciplinary design practice, while preserving professional accountability and human control over consequential decisions.
